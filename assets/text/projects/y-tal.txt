<p>y-tal (or just tal) is a Python library with many utilities for people who work in the development of non-line-of-sight imaging techniques, which attempt to <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JWDocXPy-iQ">reconstruct objects outside the line of sight</a>. This library provides different tools to generate and analyze data and implementations of different non-line-of-sight reconstruction algorithms. I typically use this library for my research: if you are interested you can also check my post on <a href="/projects/mitransient"><i>mitransient</i></a>.<br></p>
