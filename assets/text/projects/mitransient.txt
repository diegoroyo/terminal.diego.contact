<p><i>mitransient</i> is a library for transient light transport simulation. Think of a standard rendering sofware, like Mitsuba 3, which is able to generate realistic images by computing how the light interacts with every element. Our extension <i>mitransient</i> adds the time dimension to the mix, being able to track the time of flight of each photon with ultra-high resolution. As as a result, <i>mitransient</i> can show how light propagates through the scene, from being emitted by the light source to interacting with the materials and finally reaching the camera.<br><br>

In the following video we show an image of a scene. On the right we show the result of our simulation. You can see how the light in the ceiling illuminates the different wall. Photons that take longer paths also take a longer time to reach the camera and thus appear later in the video.<br></p>
